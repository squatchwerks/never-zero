      The rules

      1. A week is scored by counted days, not streaks. Aim for 5 of 7, with 2–3 at normal or better.
      2. Tired or busy means a minimum day, never a zero.
      3. Commit to five minutes. You can stop after that and it still counts.
      4. Never make up missed work.

      Restarting

      There is no streak, so nothing breaks. After any gap, the next day is a minimum day, done today and not "starting Monday."

      After two or more weeks off, do one week of minimum and normal days only. Move week in December is a minimum-only week by design.

      Habits, biggest first

      1. Daily minimum
      Costs 10 minutes. Keeps the habit alive.
      2. Protein-first eating
      Costs prep time and a less fun dinner. Most of the weight loss.
      3. Easy cardio, 2–3 times a week
      Costs about 90 minutes a week. Keep it at a pace where you can talk.
      4. Two short lifting sessions
      Costs about 40 minutes a week. Protects muscle while you cut.
      5. Staying off alcohol
      Helps sleep, calories, and the off-the-rails days.

      Nicotine raises heart rate, so judge effort by whether you can talk, not by heart-rate zones.
